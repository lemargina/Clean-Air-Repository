AI agents were used as development assistants during the creation of Clean Air Finder Bulgaria.

During planning, AI agents helped turn the project brief into an implementation plan covering the backend, data collection, interface, operations, and validation.

Research support from AI agents helped document the Sensor.Community measurement source and related geographic data sources.

For the backend, AI agents helped design the Laravel application structure and PostgreSQL schema for measurements, locations, and import progress.

Implementation assistance included services for parsing measurements, collecting current data, and importing historical archives.

The agents also helped develop city assignment, daily aggregation, coverage checks, and cleanest-city ranking logic.

On the frontend, AI assisted with building the Blade interface and HTTP endpoints for viewing air quality results.

Testing support included preparing automated tests and fixtures for measurement parsing, geographic matching, and reporting behavior.


AI agents also assisted with Docker Compose, startup scripts, scheduled jobs, and queue worker configuration.
For project documentation, AI helped describe the API, data methodology, architecture, deployment, operations, and monitoring.
Finally, AI agents helped organize implementation work into reviewable stages and propose logical commit groupings.
AI-generated suggestions and code were intended to be reviewed and validated by a human, especially for real data behavior and operational claims.
AI assistance does not mean that historical data imports, benchmarks, or production deployment were automatically completed.
